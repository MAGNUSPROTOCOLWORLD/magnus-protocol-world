Commercial Data Validation

Commercial websites may process information from many sources. Data validation helps identify incomplete fields, inconsistent values, outdated records and conflicts between sources.

External Data vs Internal Analysis

External merchant information should remain distinguishable from internally generated analysis. This separation helps prevent analytical conclusions from being mistaken for merchant statements.

Changing Information

Prices, promotions, availability, shipping conditions and merchant policies may change without notice. The merchant or affiliate network remains the authoritative source for final transaction terms.
