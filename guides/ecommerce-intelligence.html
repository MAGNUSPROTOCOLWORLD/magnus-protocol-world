E-Commerce Intelligence

E-commerce intelligence combines structured data, catalog analysis and contextual information to make large amounts of commercial information easier to understand.

Catalog Organization

Products can be grouped according to categories, attributes, merchant information and other relevant characteristics.

Opportunity Analysis

Technology may help identify interesting commercial patterns, but these patterns should not be represented as guaranteed financial opportunities.

Information Quality

Good commerce intelligence prioritizes traceability, accuracy, transparency and clear separation between external commercial data and internal analysis.
