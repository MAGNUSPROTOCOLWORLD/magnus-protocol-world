Consumer Transparency in Digital Commerce

Transparency helps consumers distinguish editorial information, technology-generated analysis, merchant claims and advertising relationships.

Clear Commercial Relationships

When affiliate compensation may be received, the relationship should be disclosed in understandable language and in a location users can reasonably find.

Accurate Representation

Publishers should avoid presenting themselves as an official representative of a merchant or network unless such authorization actually exists.

Useful Information First

A sustainable commerce website provides informational value independently of whether a user clicks an affiliate link.
