Responsible AI in Commerce

AI can assist with classification, information retrieval, catalog organization, comparison and decision support, but responsible implementation requires transparency and appropriate limitations.

Human-Centered Decisions

Users should retain control over purchasing decisions. AI output should be treated as informational support rather than an instruction to purchase a product.

Avoiding Misleading Content

Responsible commerce systems should not fabricate testimonials, scarcity, sales figures, merchant relationships or performance guarantees.

Explainable Limitations

A trustworthy system communicates that machine-generated analysis can be incomplete and that commercial terms must be verified with the applicable merchant.
