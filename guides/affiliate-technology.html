Affiliate Technology

Affiliate technology connects publishers, advertisers and consumers through trackable commercial links and authorized network infrastructure.

How Affiliate Links Work

When a publisher participates in an approved affiliate program, the network may provide tracking technology that attributes qualifying activity according to the advertiser's program terms.

Disclosure

Users should be informed when a commercial relationship may result in compensation. Affiliate disclosures improve transparency and help users understand the business model of a publisher.

No Earnings Guarantees

Affiliate participation does not guarantee commissions, revenue, traffic, conversions or financial results.
