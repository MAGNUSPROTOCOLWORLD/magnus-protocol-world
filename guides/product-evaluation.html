Product Evaluation Methodology

A useful product evaluation process should separate objective product information from analytical interpretation. Specifications, merchant descriptions, price and availability are different from internal rankings or recommendations.

Core Evaluation Factors

Potential evaluation factors include product specifications, category relevance, merchant data quality, availability signals, pricing context, product information completeness and consumer usability.

No Artificial Rankings

MAGNUSPROTOCOL.WORLD does not need to invent popularity, customer satisfaction, sales volume or conversion metrics. Rankings should only use information supported by legitimate and authorized data sources.

Transparency

When affiliate relationships exist, they should be disclosed clearly. Commercial relationships should not convert advertising claims into supposedly independent facts.
