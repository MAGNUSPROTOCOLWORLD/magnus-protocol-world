Merchant Intelligence

Merchant intelligence is the structured analysis of information associated with sellers, advertisers, catalogs, offers and commercial relationships.

It can help organize merchant data, identify catalog structure, compare publicly or contractually available commercial information and improve the presentation of products to users.

Authorized Information

Merchant intelligence should rely on legitimate sources, including information supplied directly by merchants, advertisers or authorized affiliate network integrations.

Commercial Neutrality

Analytical tools should avoid implying partnerships that do not exist. A merchant should only be represented as an active commercial partner when that relationship is actually authorized.
